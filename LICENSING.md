The Sens license covers only code and assets for which TheRofli holds the
necessary rights. Dependencies, bundled fonts and runtimes, downloaded model
packs, and third-party screenshots or reference fixtures keep their own
licenses and copyrights. Nothing in the Sens license relicenses that material.
